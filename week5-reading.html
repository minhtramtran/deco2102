Organising the Content

For Week 5, we will focus on how information is organised and structured so people can understand what is available and find what they need.

Chapter 2
Organizing the Content: Information Architecture and Application Structure
Book
Designing Interfaces: Patterns for Effective Interaction Design
Authors
Jenifer Tidwell, Charles Brewer and Aynne Valencia

Within the scope of this semester, and particularly for your first interface design project, the information architecture problems you work with are unlikely to be very complex. But I would still like you to have a good foundation for thinking about how information and functionality should be organised.

You only need to read from the beginning of Chapter 2 through Do: Facilitate a Single Task. You are welcome to explore the remainder of the chapter.
