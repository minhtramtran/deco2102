We’re changing the visual design of this page each week to make the reading feel a bit more engaging, while keeping the content structure consistent. This week’s theme is the chunky, big-button style of BIG Launcher, an Android home screen for seniors. Here, seniors means older adults, commonly defined in Australia as people aged 65 and over.

The principles behind this design

1. Readable everywhereShneiderman notes that systems let elderly users enlarge text or contrast in documents but "rarely" in control panels, help messages or dialogue boxes. Here every control gets the large type.Source: Shneiderman (2000), Universal Usability
2. Big touch targetsThe strictest WCAG level asks for targets of at least 44×44px. Every button on these screens is at least 84px tall.Source: WCAG 2.2, 2.5.5 Target Size (AAA)
3. Clear edgesButton outlines need at least 3:1 contrast with what's around them. The black outlines here reach about 15:1 against the lavender.Source: WCAG 2.2, 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (AA)
4. High-contrast textThe strictest WCAG level asks for 7:1 contrast for regular text. All text on these screens meets it.Source: WCAG 2.2, 1.4.6 Contrast (AAA)
